ANI Scratch AI is a small language model created for learning.
This model begins with randomly initialized parameters. It learns by predicting the next byte in text.
A language model learns patterns from examples. It does not understand the world like a person.
The goal is to build, train, inspect, and improve our own small Transformer on a Windows computer.
We can improve the model by adding clean, varied text that we have permission to use.
